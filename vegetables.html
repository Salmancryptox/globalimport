How Farm Pre-Booking Works

A simple sourcing process between farms, Global Import and international buyers.

01

02

Global Import

We review the crop information and explore potential international buyer requirements.

03

Buyer Enquiry

An interested buyer can request a quotation for the required vegetable, quantity and destination.

04

Trade & Dispatch

Once commercial terms are agreed, the relevant parties can coordinate harvesting, preparation and export arrangements.
